## Brief items that turned out different, or needed a changed hypothesis

1. **(c)/(d) "components of period coprime to $P$ average out by CRT".** True but too weak. The exact statement is: the fold of $K_m$ to modulus $P$ is $K_m$ if $m\mid P$ and $0$ if $m\nmid P$ (L9.2), e.g. $K_4$ folds to $0$ at $P=2$ although $\gcd(4,2)\ne1$. Checked for $P\le24$, $m\le16$.
2. **(d) the "drift" example $\theta=2\pi/13\cdot(1+10^{-3})$ is not a T9d instance.** $\theta/2\pi=1.001/13=77/1000$ exactly, so in exact arithmetic the kernel is 1000-periodic and T5's hypothesis fails ($c\cdot\theta\in2\pi(\mathbb Q\setminus\mathbb Z)$ for $c=1$). The numerical conclusion is unchanged, but the right theorem is T9c (the fold to modulus 13 is the constant, because $\gcd(1000,13)=1$). The double the script actually evaluates is a rational number of radians, hence an irrational multiple of $2\pi$ (T5 holds for it literally), but that is an artefact of floating point; the draft says so and adds the irrational near-resonant kernel as a clean T9d example.
3. **(a) rate.** Done as asked, with two sharpenings: the a.s. rate per comb vote is exactly $(p-\nu)\log\rho$, the same for every bounded $b$ (pointwise $e^{-B}\mathrm{NLL}^{\rm Bayes}\le\mathrm{NLL}\le e^{B}\mathrm{NLL}^{\rm Bayes}$); the bound on the *expectation* decays at the slower rate $-\log\chi$ ($0.3636$ vs $1.4166$ per vote for $V=16$). The expectation bound is not claimed to be tight.
4. **(b) default bank.** As expected, $D_{12}$ and $D_{13}$ lie in the span of $\{K_1,\dots,K_{16}\}$. New point beyond the brief: if an evidence kernel reuses the repo's softmax-simplex weights ($\alpha_m>0$ for all $m$ in the bank), then $D_P$ is reached *exactly* only when the bank equals the divisor set of $P$; in the default bank it is only approximated. Which parametrization the evidence head uses is a design question for the E track.
5. **(b) cosines.** All three asked claims hold: prime $P\ge5$ not in $\mathrm{span}\{1,\cos(2\pi n/P)\}$; $D_{12}$ not in the span of the matched cosines (harmonic $a=5$ missing, harmonics $0,1,2,3,4,6$ present); in the span of all harmonics $a=0..6$. Stronger result: $D_P$ is in the span of fundamental cosines of *any* orders iff $\varphi(P)\le2$ iff $P\in\{1,2,3,4,6\}$. The brief's remark "$\cos(2\pi an/P)$ is a fundamental cosine iff $a\mid P$" is correct only for $1\le a\le\lfloor P/2\rfloor$ (for larger $a$ replace $a$ by $P-a$); the constant $a=0$ is separate. $P=1$ is trivially in the span.
6. **(e) copies break the Bayes equality.** True and exact (mean top probability 8/9 vs argmax accuracy 2/3 at $V=3,m=1,k=2$, a computed fact about that enumeration; expected NLL excess $=\mathbb E\,\mathrm{KL}>0$ in every row). The head's NLL still decays exponentially in the number of *distinct* votes $m$ for fixed $k$ ($\le k(V-1)\chi^m$): what fails is the Bayes equality and any uniform-in-$k$ guarantee (expected loss grows at least linearly in $k$). The brief's word for this was withdrawn from the draft in round 3 (see "Revision after audit").
7. **(b) bank-design observation (from the script).** Confirmed and made precise: $B_{16}\cup\{1\}$ has $D_P$ in its span exactly for the prime powers $2,3,4,5,7,8,9,11,13,16$ (P ≤ 40), while T4 selects the combs $\mathrm{lcm}(S)\mathbb Z$ for many more periods (6, 10, 12, 14, 15, 18, … ). For $P=12$, the best CRT choice among two-order mixtures, $S=\{3,4\}$ with $a=\frac12$ (not the best element of the span; round 3: no element of $\mathrm{span}\{K_1,\dots,K_4\}$ is dominant), gives $b=\frac12(K_3+K_4)$ whose off-comb values sum in modulus to 4, so by T9c.5 some motif has negative linear rate in an unnormalized sum (slope −1/8 in units of $\lambda$). Exact counting needs a divisor-closed bank (or at least all divisors of the $P$ of interest); softmax comb selection needs only the lcm. Also: $B_{16}$ *without* order 1 (the 10-order bank of MATH.md) misses $D_{13}$ because $D_{13}=\frac1{13}K_1+\frac{12}{13}K_{13}$ needs the constant.

No item of the brief was disproved.

## What I did not prove / open questions

- A **finite-$i$ rate** for T9d. Weyl gives only $o(i)$; a rate needs Diophantine information on $\theta$. The noise term is $O(\sqrt{i\log i})$ a.s. (proved).
- The **prefactor** of $\chi^{n}$ in the expected NLL (T9a.3). The exponent is proved tight in round 3 (T9a.5); the $1/\sqrt n$ prefactor is only observed numerically.
- For kernels other than $D_P$ and for $W\ne I$, a full characterization of when a shared $W$ gives positive margin for all motifs. T9c.5 solves the case $W=I$ (strict dominance of the fold); the general case is a question about the cone of motifs and is OPEN.
- **Unknown or mixed periods**, and the interaction of $E_i$ with deeper layers (T8/C8 are about networks without this head).
- The "only if" part of T9b.1 for **finite contexts** $N<L$ (independence of the $K_m$ may fail on a window shorter than $\mathrm{lcm}$).
- T9e treats exact copies at comb positions only. Weaker dependence is not analyzed.
- Nothing is claimed about learnability, trained rates, natural data, speed, linear attention, or novelty (T9f). References in `T9_DRAFT.md` carry "(unverified)" where I could not confirm details offline; I did not use the network.

## Suggestions for the other tracks

- The E track should decide whether the evidence kernel uses free coefficients (span; then divisors of $P$ in the bank suffice) or the softmax simplex (then exact $D_P$ requires bank = divisors of $P$); see item 4. Whichever is chosen, the bank must contain order 1.
- For a dev pilot at $P=12$ the default bank $\{1,\dots,16\}$ is fine; $B_{16}$ is not (T9b.2). For $P=13$, $B_{16}$ needs order 1 added.
- To see the T9c/T9d separation empirically one needs a motif with at least two distinct symbols (a constant motif is not separated, T9d remark (i)).
