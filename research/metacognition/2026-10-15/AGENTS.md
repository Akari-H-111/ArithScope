# Isolated metacognition study

Read `README.md`, `PROTOCOL.md`, and `HANDOFF.md` here before continuing.

- All work belongs below this directory. Do not change the parent library,
  experiment suites, preregistrations, claim ledger, or results.
- Do not check out another branch, stage, commit, push, or contact another chat
  as part of this study without a new instruction from the owner.
- Parent E9 may be running. Use one CPU thread, low process priority, and no
  long training or long-context backward passes.
- `inputs/` contains frozen copies of two completed E6 checkpoints and their
  original run records. Verify `inputs/manifest.json` before using them.
- Results here are development diagnostics, exact finite checks, or numerical
  checks. They do not amend the parent's confirmatory claims.
- Keep outputs immutable: write a new version for a changed protocol or source.
  Record deviations in this directory's `PROTOCOL.md`.
- All reviewable files are English. Retain failed logs and explicit limits.
